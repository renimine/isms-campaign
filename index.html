4 Steps to Secure Your Daily Workflow

Follow official IT guidelines to keep corporate access secure and compliant.
"Use approved tools only! Stay safe!"
STEP 01 Use Approved MFA Only
Third-party authenticator apps and browser MFA extensions are strictly prohibited.
STEP 02 Remove Unapproved Extensions
Uninstall unauthorized browser add-ons and password auto-fill extensions immediately.
STEP 03 Store Keys Securely
Never save corporate tokens or credentials inside browser local stores.
STEP 04 Report Policy Violations
Reach out to the IT Security Desk if you need assistance configuring approved MFA devices.
